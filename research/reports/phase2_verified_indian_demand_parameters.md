## 1. Study Methodology & National Sampling Frame

- **Sampling Design**: Cross-sectional stratified multi-stage survey across 5 randomly selected states representing geographical regions of India.
- **Facilities Sampled**: 251 healthcare facilities (public and private, primary, secondary, and tertiary care).
- **Bed Denominator Sampled**: 51,562 total staffed hospital beds across sampled facilities.
- **Total Observed Clinical Demand**: **474,627 whole blood units** across the 251 facilities.
- **Unit Standard Definition**: In Indian transfusion practice, one standard whole blood donation unit is **350 mL** (or 450 mL in higher-weight donors; national standard calibration = 350 mL whole blood unit equivalent).
- **Extrapolated National Population**: Extrapolated to eligible Indian donor/recipient population (~402 million eligible population).

### Key Clinical Observations from Table 5:
1. **Severe Platelet Vulnerability**: Platelets show the highest unmet deficit nationwide, with supply covering only **77.5%** of clinical demand (a **22.5% structural deficit**). This is heavily driven by short biological shelf-life (5 days at 20°C–24°C under continuous agitation).
2. **Plasma Wastage & Fractionation Gap**: Plasma exhibits an **86.0% supply coverage** and a relatively high loss rate before clinical utilization (3.2%), representing significant missed potential for inter-bank redistribution.
3. **High RBC Utilization Under Scarcity**: Red cells achieve **99.2% utilization of available supply**, indicating that hospital blood banks operate in a state of chronic demand pressure where almost every available unit is transfused.

## 6. Mathematical Simulator Calibration Formulae

For our multi-bank network simulator (Option 1: 5 banks, 12 hospitals), hospital baseline daily demand $\bar{D}_{h,c}$ for hospital $h$ and component $c$ is deterministically initialized using these verified empirical values:

$$\bar{D}_{h,c} = \frac{\text{Beds}_h \times \text{BOR}_h \times \lambda^{\text{tier}}_h \times \phi_c \times \sum_{s} (\omega_{h,s} \cdot \beta_{s,c})}{365}$$

Where:
- $\text{Beds}_h$: Total registered acute beds for hospital $h$.
- $\text{BOR}_h$: Empirically verified Bed Occupancy Rate from Section 4 (83.9% for Tertiary, 72.9% for Secondary, 60.4% for Primary).
- $\lambda^{\text{tier}}_h$: BOR-adjusted annual demand factor (19.8 for Tertiary/Trauma, 8.4 for Secondary, 2.1 for Primary).
- $\phi_c$: Component normalization multiplier ensuring national supply-demand balance aligns with Table 5.
- $\omega_{h,s}$: Bed allocation share of clinical specialty $s \in \{\text{Medicine}, \text{Surgery}, \text{OB/GYN}, \text{Pediatrics}\}$ at hospital $h$.
- $\beta_{s,c}$: Specialty-to-component transfusion fraction derived from Section 5.
